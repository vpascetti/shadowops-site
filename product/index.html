Operational Domains

Core Modules

  • Items with inventory, BOM, routing, traceability, and barcode support
  • Sales Orders with linked work orders, shipment risk, and timeline tracking
  • Purchase Orders with supplier metrics, receipts, and vendor recommendations
  • Work Orders with production execution, WIP, scrap, and downtime logging
  • Machines with real-time events, utilization, and root-cause history

Platform Principles

  • AI is advisory, never autonomous
  • All state changes are explicit and auditable
  • Single codebase supports cloud, on-premise, hybrid, and edge
  • Multi-tenant by design with org and plant scoping
  • Provider-agnostic AI supports OpenAI, Azure, and local models
