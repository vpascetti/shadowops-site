Security and Control by Design.

ShadowOps is built for manufacturing operations that need AI guidance, data isolation, and deployment flexibility without sacrificing governance.

Advisory AI only

ShadowOps generates recommendations and insight cards, but people approve the final action.

Least-privilege connectivity

Access is scoped to what is required for operational visibility, with controls aligned to your organization.

Customer-controlled deployment

Run ShadowOps in cloud, on-premise, hybrid, or edge environments based on your security model.

No ERP replacement

ShadowOps sits above your systems of record to improve visibility and workflow speed, not replace them.

Data minimization

Only the operational data needed to support the configured workflow and advisory layer is processed.

Audit trail and RBAC

All recommendations, approvals, and actions are recorded with role-based access control and tenant scoping.
