Built for Modern Manufacturing Operations.

ShadowOps exists to help manufacturers coordinate plants, streamline ERP workflows, and give teams a trustworthy operating layer for real-world decisions.

Why We Built It

The problem

Manufacturers already have data, but it is often fragmented across ERP, plant systems, and spreadsheets. Teams still spend too much time translating records into action.

ShadowOps was designed as a production-grade operating layer that combines live visibility, advisory AI, and role-aware workflows around the systems manufacturers already trust.

  • One operating model across cloud, on-premise, hybrid, and edge deployments
  • AI guidance that stays advisory, grounded, and auditable
  • Multi-plant coordination with local plant control
  • Offline resilience for critical manufacturing environments

The goal is straightforward: help manufacturers make faster, better decisions without forcing a rip-and-replace ERP project.

Our Mission

Make Operations Visible

Give teams a live command center for production, supply, and machine status.

Turn Data Into Guidance

Provide context-aware advisory insights that are grounded in the record the user is already viewing.

Respect Human Control

Keep AI advisory, decisions approved by people, and all actions auditable.

What We Believe

Read-Only by Default

ShadowOps is designed to observe and recommend, not silently mutate the systems of record.

No Hidden Magic

Domain models, validation, and audit trails should stay explicit and understandable to operators and developers alike.

Practical Deployment

Manufacturers need cloud, on-premise, hybrid, and edge options without changing how the platform works.
