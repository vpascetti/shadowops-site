Pilot Qualification Checklist

Best fit: manufacturers that want a fast path to real operational visibility and advisory workflows.

  • Need for a live command center across one or more plants
  • Interest in AI advisory that stays human-approved
  • Multiple operational domains to validate, such as work orders or inventory
  • Interest in multi-tenant or plant-scoped governance
  • Interest in offline resilience or edge processing
  • Team willing to review workflows and provide operational feedback
  • Access to ERP or plant data for read-only validation
  • Decision-maker available to align on pilot goals and rollout path
  • Leadership sponsor focused on measurable operational outcomes
  • Will to evaluate cloud, on-premise, hybrid, or edge deployment needs

Common Questions

Do you access my ERP data?

Yes, read-only access only. ShadowOps never modifies your ERP or systems without explicit user approval.

What if it doesn't fit?

The pilot is meant to validate fit quickly. If ShadowOps is not delivering clear value, we stop with no long-term commitment.

Can I try it without committing?

Yes, the pilot is the trial. You can validate value before deciding on a broader rollout.

What happens after the pilot?

If the pilot works, we move to an implementation or rollout plan based on your deployment model and priority workflows.

Do you support our environment?

We support cloud, on-premise, hybrid, and edge approaches, and can discuss the best fit during kickoff.

How much data do you need?

We start with the minimum needed to validate the workflow and expand as the pilot matures.

Ready to scope your pilot?

Start with a short fit call and map one workflow to validate first.
