How It Works

From setup to plant execution in practical phases.

ShadowOps ERP is designed for phased implementation so teams can adopt core workflows without overwhelming production.

Step 1: Foundation

Configure company structure, users, plants, customers, items, machines, BOMs, and routings.

Step 2: Production Pilot

Activate scheduling, work orders, and floor execution in a focused plant workflow.

Step 3: Inventory and Purchasing

Connect receiving, allocations, shortages, and supplier visibility to active production.

Step 4: Quality and Shipping

Roll in inspections, holds, corrective actions, and shipment readiness controls.

Step 5: Optimization

Expand to advanced reporting, multi-plant controls, and operational financial visibility.
